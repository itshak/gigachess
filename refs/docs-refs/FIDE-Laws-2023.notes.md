# FIDE Laws 2023 — extract for purechess

Source: https://handbook.fide.com/files/handbook/LawsOfChess2023.pdf
Effective: 2023-01-01
Articles relevant to purechess: 3.1-3.8 (piece moves), 3.7 (castling), 3.9 (en passant), 9.2/9.3 (repetition), 5.1/5.2 (check/mate), Appendix C (SAN), Appendix F (Chess960).

Castling 960: king and rook may start on any square; after O-O/O-O-O king ends on g/c file, rook on f/d.
FEN spec: 6 fields — piece placement, active color, castling, ep, halfmove, fullmove.
